I'm a Biomedical and Electrical Engineering dual-degree student at Colorado State University working at the intersection of medical imaging and machine learning. Right now, I'm a student researcher in CSU's Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT) Lab, where I help build real-time diagnostic tools for infant intubation, as roughly 40% of initial tube placements go wrong. I design and train deep learning models that read EIT video and tell physicians whether a tube is where it should be, all non-invasively and without radiation.

Before that, I built a 4-electrode EIT device from scratch as a sophomore project. My teammates and I designed the multiplexing circuit and I wrote the full Python control stack. Then, I validated the device with over 144,000 voltage measurements, presenting the hardware validation research at the 2026 CSU Undergraduate Research Conference. It was one of six projects chosen to demo to the ECE Industry Advisory Board.

Outside the lab, I'm president of Writer's Society, CSU's *premier* creative writing club, and have had short fiction published. Creative writing allows me to envision an entire world full of life and innovation (see my work-in-progress novel, Interfaced) that goes hand in hand with creative engineering solutions. I also tinker with technology for fun: flashing Libreboot onto an old ThinkPad, self-hosting my own website and email, and am currently building a cheap EIT system based off a HardwareX publication.

I'm graduating in May 2029 and am interested in roles where I can keep combining electronics, signal processing, ML, and biomedical science to solve problems that matter to people.
